# Privacy Policy — Sudoku

**Effective date:** 1 September 2026
**Last updated:** 1 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile application **Sudoku** (the "App",
Android package `org.hcj.sudoku`) handles information. The App is published by
**Holash Chand**, an individual developer ("we", "us", "our").

Contact / grievance officer: **loganbabu87@gmail.com**

---

## 1. Summary

- The App has **no account, no login, and no server of ours**. We do not receive
  any data about you.
- All game data (progress, statistics, streaks, in-game coins, settings, owned
  themes) is stored **only on your device**.
- The App shows ads through **Google AdMob**. To do this, Google and its ad
  partners collect and process a limited set of data described in Section 3.
- The App checks for updates through the app store. No gameplay data is
  transmitted.

## 2. Data we collect ourselves

**None.** We operate no backend, analytics, crash-reporting, or telemetry
service. We cannot identify you and never receive your game data.

Data created while you play is written to your device's local app storage
(`shared_preferences`) and never leaves the device except by an action you take
(for example, a device-level backup you have enabled with your OS provider).

## 3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App displays interstitial and rewarded video ads using the Google Mobile Ads
SDK. When an ad is requested, **Google and its ad technology partners** may
collect and process:

| Data | Purpose | Shared with |
|---|---|---|
| Advertising ID (Android Advertising ID / AAID) | Serve and measure ads, frequency capping, limited ad personalisation | Google, ad partners |
| Approximate (coarse) location derived from IP address | Ad relevance and legal/regional compliance | Google, ad partners |
| Device information (model, OS version, language, network type) | Ad delivery and fraud prevention | Google, ad partners |
| Ad interaction events (impressions, clicks) | Ad measurement and billing | Google, ad partners |

This processing is governed by Google's own terms, not ours:

- Google Privacy Policy — https://policies.google.com/privacy
- How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services —
  https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
- Google Advertising policies — https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

We do **not** sell or "share" (as defined by U.S. state privacy laws) your
personal information for money. Ad serving is the only disclosure of data and it
is handled by Google as described above.

### Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland)

Where required, the App presents a **Google-certified consent message (Google
User Messaging Platform)** before ads load, allowing you to accept or decline
personalised advertising and to exercise your choices under the GDPR / UK GDPR.
You can reopen these choices at any time from **Settings → Legal → Ad privacy
choices** inside the App.

### Families / children

The App is **not directed to children** and is rated for a general audience
(target age 13+). We do not knowingly collect data from children. Ads are
requested with a maximum content rating suitable for a teen audience and without
child-directed treatment flags. If you believe a child has used the App, reset
the Advertising ID (Section 6) and contact us.

## 4. Update checks

- **Android:** the App uses the Google Play in-app update mechanism, which
  communicates with the Play Store, not with us.
- **iOS:** the App makes an anonymous request to Apple's public
  `itunes.apple.com/lookup` endpoint to compare version numbers. No identifiers
  are sent.

## 5. Permissions

| Android permission | Why |
|---|---|
| `INTERNET`, `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE` | Load ads and check for updates |
| `VIBRATE` | Optional haptic feedback (toggle in Settings) |
| `com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID` | Access the Advertising ID for ads |

The App requests **no** access to location services, storage, camera,
microphone, contacts, calendar, phone, or SMS.

## 6. Your choices and rights

**Everyone:**

- **Reset or delete your Advertising ID:** device **Settings → Privacy → Ads**
  (Android) or **Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking / Apple Advertising**
  (iOS).
- **Delete all App data:** uninstall the App. This permanently removes every
  local record. "Reset statistics" in **Settings** also clears stats, coins and
  progression on demand.
- **Opt out of personalised ads:** use the in-App consent controls (EEA/UK) or
  your device's "Opt out of Ads Personalisation" setting.

**EEA / UK (GDPR, UK GDPR).** Our legal basis for showing non-personalised ads
and checking for updates is our **legitimate interest** in operating a free App.
Personalised ads rely on your **consent**, collected via the consent message.
You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, or object to processing,
and to data portability, in respect of data held by Google — exercise these
through Google's privacy tools linked in Section 3. You may lodge a complaint
with your local supervisory authority.

**California (CCPA / CPRA).** We do not sell or share your personal information
for monetary value. Categories "collected" (via Google) in the last 12 months:
identifiers (Advertising ID), internet/network activity (ad interactions),
approximate geolocation. You have the right to know, delete, and correct, and to
non-discrimination for exercising these rights. Submit requests to
loganbabu87@gmail.com; because we hold no data ourselves, we will direct you to
Google's controls.

**India (Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023).** You are a Data
Principal. The App's processing is limited to what is described above. You may
contact our grievance officer at **loganbabu87@gmail.com** to raise a concern; we
will respond within the statutory period. Where processing depends on consent
(personalised ads in applicable regions), you may withdraw it via the in-App
controls.

## 7. Data retention

We retain **nothing**. Local data persists on your device until you clear it or
uninstall. Data processed by Google for ads is retained per Google's own
retention schedules.

## 8. International transfers

Any data processed by Google for advertising may be transferred to and processed
in the United States and other countries under Google's data-transfer
safeguards. We do not carry out any transfers ourselves.

## 9. Security

Because we store no data on servers, there is no central database to breach.
Data transmitted for ads and update checks is sent over encrypted (HTTPS/TLS)
connections. On-device data is protected by your operating system's app
sandboxing and any device encryption you have enabled.

## 10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected by a new "Last
updated" date and, where significant, an in-App or store notice. Continued use
after an update constitutes acceptance.
